Privacy Policy

Workout Intelligence · Last updated: 17 November 2026

Workout Intelligence ("the App", "we", "us") is a personal workout planning and tracking application. This policy explains what data the App accesses when you connect your WHOOP account, how that data is used, and the choices you have.

1. Data we access

When you authorize the App through WHOOP's OAuth flow, we may access only the data covered by the scopes you approve, which can include:

  • Basic profile information (name, email, user ID)
  • Body measurements (height, weight, max heart rate)
  • Recovery, strain, and cycle data
  • Sleep data
  • Workout data (activity type, duration, heart rate, strain)

We never receive or store your WHOOP password.

2. How we use your data

Your WHOOP data is used solely to provide features within the App, such as displaying recovery and strain alongside your training sessions, informing workout planning, and showing your training history. We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to the App's functionality.

3. Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties. Data is only disclosed if required by law.

4. Storage and security

OAuth access and refresh tokens and any synchronized WHOOP data are stored securely and used only to operate the App. Reasonable technical measures are taken to protect your information from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse.

5. Retention and deletion

Data is kept only as long as your WHOOP account remains connected to the App. You can disconnect at any time from your WHOOP account settings or from within the App; upon disconnection, stored tokens are revoked and your WHOOP data is deleted. You may also request deletion at any time by contacting us.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live (including under the EU General Data Protection Regulation), you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data, and to withdraw consent at any time. To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below.

7. Children

The App is not directed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

9. Contact

For questions about this policy or your data, please open an issue at github.com/antoniomariac/whoop/issues.
